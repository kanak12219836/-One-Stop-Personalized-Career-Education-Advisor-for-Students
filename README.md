# -One-Stop-Personalized-Career-Education-Advisor-for-Students
About Our platform offers personalized career and education guidance for post-10th/12th students. It uses aptitude testing, course-to-career mapping, and a government college directory with AI recommendations to help students make informed academic choices.

One-Stop, Customized Career and Education Advisor of student.

A machine learning (AI) system which offers post-10 th and 12 th students with customized career and college advice based on aptitude assessment, course-to-career matching, and government college data.

Features

Stream Recommendation based on aptitude. Recommends Science, Commerce, Arts or other streams according to the abilities of students.

Dropout Risk Prediction Determines academically and socio-economically at risk students.

AI College Recommendation Places students in the right college based on marks, cutoffs and preferences.

College Directory Government College. Streams, cutoffs, scholarships and locations are centralized databases.

Tech Stack

Python Pandas, NumPy Scikit-learn, LightGBM Matplotlib, Seaborn

How It Works

Combine student & college data. Clean and engineer features Train ML models for: Stream recommendation Dropout prediction Admission probability Build customized college recommendations.

Impact Enables students in drawing empirical decisions on academic matters, and lessens confusion and career orientation.
